# Social media kit / Materi media sosial

Dibuat oleh Ariana Mischa Fadhila dari Syubadubin Studios.

Six ready-to-post images with suggested captions. Images 01–04 are **key art illustrations** generated with GPT Image (Rodin MCP) from our own hero designs; they are labelled on the image as not being gameplay. Image 05 uses the in-game hero portraits, and 06 uses real screenshots from the game. Rebuild them with `python scripts/marketing.py` after changing the art or the screenshots; the source key art is in `assets/marketing/`.

Enam gambar siap unggah beserta usulan caption. Gambar 01–04 adalah **ilustrasi key art** yang dibuat dengan GPT Image (Rodin MCP) dari desain hero kita sendiri; pada gambarnya tertulis bahwa itu bukan cuplikan permainan. Buat ulang dengan `python scripts/marketing.py` setelah mengubah gambar atau screenshot; key art sumbernya ada di `assets/marketing/`.

## Notes before posting / Catatan sebelum mengunggah

- Keep the "key art, not gameplay" line on images 01–04, and use 06 when a post makes claims about how the game looks. / Pertahankan keterangan "bukan cuplikan permainan" pada 01–04, dan pakai 06 bila unggahan menyatakan tampilan permainan.
- Several platforms ask creators to disclose AI-generated imagery; the key art and the hero portraits were made with GPT Image. Check each platform's current rules. / Beberapa platform meminta pengungkapan gambar buatan AI; key art dan potret hero dibuat dengan GPT Image. Periksa aturan terbaru tiap platform.
- Do not add licensed characters, logos or music to posts. Background music for videos must be our own or properly licensed; the dialogue voices are on a non-commercial plan (see [ASSETS.md](../ASSETS.md)), so do not use them in paid promotion. / Jangan menambahkan tokoh, logo, atau musik berlisensi. Suara dialog memakai paket non-komersial, jadi jangan dipakai dalam promosi berbayar.
- The game is a playable prototype; avoid promising features listed as not implemented in [Progress.md](../../Progress.md). / Game ini masih prototipe; hindari menjanjikan fitur yang tercatat belum ada.
